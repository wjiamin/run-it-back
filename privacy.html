Privacy

Last updated 28 September 2026

What's saved, and where

Your covers (the video link, title, tempo, trimmed part, practice settings and where you left off) are saved in your browser's storage on your own device. They are never sent to us, and there is no account. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

Share links

When you share a practice, everything is inside the link itself: the video, its title, the beat, the part and, if you choose, your practice plan. Never where you got to. It's after the "#" in the link, so it isn't sent to any server, including ours. It only goes where you send it. We count that a link was made or opened, but not what's in it.

The debug log

The Log button keeps a short technical log on your device, to help fix problems. It never includes video links or titles, and it only leaves your device if you copy it and send it to someone yourself.

Visit counts

We count visits with GoatCounter, so we know how many people use Run It Back and which features help. It uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites. For each visit it records the page, the site you came from, your browser, screen size and country. Your IP address is used only to work out the country and isn't stored. It also counts a few moments in the app: a video loaded, practice started or finished, the app installed, a share link made or opened, and the tip link pressed. Never which video, or anything you typed.

Tips

The tip link opens a separate site that handles the payment under its own privacy policy. Nothing about your practice is sent to it.

YouTube

Videos play through YouTube's embedded player, and the pictures in My covers are YouTube's own video thumbnails. So YouTube receives the usual information any embedded video gets (such as your IP address and the video you're watching) and may set its own cookies. See Google's privacy policy.

Working offline

If you install the app, a copy of its own files is kept on your device so it opens without a connection. Videos still need the internet.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if we add ads), this page will say so first, with the date updated above.
