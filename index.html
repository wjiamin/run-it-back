    How it works

    1. Load the video. It plays mirrored, with speed control, and goes full screen.
    2. Set the beat: a rough tempo, then mark two 1s so the app can count for you.
    3. It learns the whole song in 8-count blocks, or trim it to just a part.
    4. Press Start. Each block starts on a 5-6-7-8 count-in and plays slow, then faster, then full speed, with a short pause after each run. Every few blocks are connected together, and then the whole section runs with the music.
    5. As you add blocks, everything so far runs from the top. Messed up a run? Press Again. Got it already? Press Next to skip to the next speed. Next time, you can continue where you left off.

    Use it like an app

    On iPhone or iPad: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen, without Safari's bars, and starts even when you're offline.

    Step 2 · Mark the 1 where the dance starts

    Play to the first count of the choreography and press the button. Most exact: pause right on the 1, step with the arrows below, then press.

    Step 3 · Mark a later 1

    Skip ahead to another 1. The further away the better: near the end of the song is best. The app counts the 8-counts in between and works out the exact tempo.

    Step 4 · Check it

    Turn on Flash or Click under the video and play. The edge flashes on every count (green on the 1), and the click marks each count. If it's a little early or late, use Timing below. If the 1 is on the wrong beat, redo step 2.
