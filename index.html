    How it works

    1. Load the video. It plays mirrored, with speed control, and goes full screen.
    2. Tap along to the beat once so the app can count for you.
    3. Trim the part you want to learn. The app cuts it into 8-count blocks.
    4. Press Start. Each block starts on a 5-6-7-8 count-in and plays slow, then faster, then full speed, with a short pause after each run. Every few blocks are connected together, and then the whole section runs with the music.
