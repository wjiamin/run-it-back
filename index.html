    Step 1 · Rough tempo

    Press Listen with the sound on (not in headphones) and the app works out the tempo. Or tap along to about 8 beats (the video starts at half speed, which makes it easier), or type the BPM. It only needs to be close: step 3 makes it exact.

    Step 2 · Mark the 1 where the dance starts

    Play to the first count of the choreography and press the button. Most exact: pause right on the 1, step with the arrows below, then press.

    Step 3 · Mark a later 1

    Skip ahead to another 1. The further away the better: near the end of the song is best. The app counts the 8-counts in between and works out the exact tempo.

    Move the video

    Step 4 · Check it

    Turn on Flash or Click under the video and play. The edge flashes on every count (green on the 1), and the click marks each count. If it's a little early or late, use Timing below. If the 1 is on the wrong beat, redo step 2.

    Record yourself

    Dance along to the video while your camera records you, then watch yourself next to the dancers to see what to fix. Prop your phone up so you're in the picture.

    ⏺ Record counts down 3 seconds, then plays the video from where it is, at the speed you've chosen. It stops when you press ■ Stop or pause the video. To start somewhere else, move the video there first. Only your latest recording is kept, on this phone, until you close the video.
